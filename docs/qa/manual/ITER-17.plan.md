## 6) Risks / assumptions / explicitly out of scope
- Risks: many ITER-17 pages are currently `ui-static` in traceability; converting to concrete APIs can break openapi/traceability/visual gates unless docs+backend+frontend are updated atomically.
- Assumptions: tenant is derived from JWT/headers only; all write endpoints enforce `Idempotency-Key`; settlement/payout/agreement changes are append-only auditable with `request_id` and no PII logging.
- Out of scope (ITER-17): real payment-channel remittance integration, real invoice/tax provider integration, and non-ITER-17 page redesigns outside listed `page_key` scope.
