## 6) Risks / assumptions / explicitly out of scope
- Risks: risk/insurance/blacklist pages are currently `ui-static` in traceability and absent in OpenAPI paths; updates must be atomic across backend + openapi + traceability + codegen outputs to keep gates green.
- Assumptions: tenant is derived from JWT/request context only, all high-risk writes require `Idempotency-Key`, audit records remain append-only with `request_id`, and PII stays masked in logs/API/UI unless privileged.
- Out of scope: real insurer webhook/signature integration, external payout/claim settlement, and redesign of non-ITER-19 pages.
